01 / WHY WE COLLECT

More than music.
It's the ritual.

Here at The Crate Diaries, we celebrate the experience of collecting vinyl records — from the first spin to the unexpected record-store find.

Streaming gives us everything at once. Vinyl asks us to slow down, listen closely, and make room for the albums that become part of our story. This is a place to learn, discover, and share the hunt.
